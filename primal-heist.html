Primal Heist

Primal Heist is a cooperative prehistoric action-adventure game for PC built around teamwork, fast escapes, physical comedy and unpredictable situations. Players control a distinctive cast of animal characters and work together through dangerous environments where simple objectives can quickly turn into chaotic chases.
