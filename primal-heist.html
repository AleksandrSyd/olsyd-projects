Primal Heist

Primal Heist is a cooperative prehistoric action-adventure game for PC built around teamwork, fast escapes, physical comedy and unpredictable situations. Players take control of a distinctive cast of animal characters and work together through dangerous environments where a simple objective can quickly turn into a chaotic chase.

Targeting February 20271–4 PlayersWindows PCIn Development
The Game

Cooperative prehistoric chaos

Primal Heist is designed around compact, memorable cooperative sessions. Players explore stylized prehistoric environments, complete objectives, improvise when plans fail and escape oversized threats together. The goal is not only to succeed, but to create the kind of unexpected moments that become stories between friends after the session ends.

Players

1–4 Player Co-op

Designed for solo play and cooperative sessions with up to four players.

Genre

Action Adventure

Traversal, objectives, escapes, vehicles and environmental challenges form the core loop.

Setting

Stylized Prehistory

Canyons, deserts, jungles, snow regions and prehistoric threats create varied scenarios.

Core Experience

Built for memorable sessions with friends

Teamwork under pressure

Objectives are designed to encourage cooperation while leaving room for mistakes, improvisation and last-second saves.

A recognizable cast

The game centers on expressive animal characters with a consistent visual identity and strong personality.

Movement and escape

Running, driving, gliding and surviving chase sequences are central to the game's rhythm and spectacle.

Focused session design

The experience is being developed around relatively compact play sessions that deliver action without requiring a long commitment every time.

Release target: Primal Heist is currently targeting a February 2027 release window for PC. The schedule and final feature set may evolve during production.
