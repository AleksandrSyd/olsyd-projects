Screen Troublemaker

Screen Troublemaker is a character-driven interactive desktop experience that turns the computer screen itself into the stage. The animated character appears to live alongside the user's everyday desktop — moving around, reacting, resting, playing and creating unexpected moments while the computer remains usable.

Targeting Early September 2026Windows PCIn Development
What it is

A character that feels present on your screen

Screen Troublemaker is designed as a persistent animated presence with personality, body language and behavior. The concept allows the character to appear to interact with windows, icons, the cursor and open desktop space, making the screen itself part of the experience.

Type

Desktop Entertainment

Designed to coexist with normal computer use rather than replace it with a full-screen game.
